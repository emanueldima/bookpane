# App icon

Edited with the built-in `image_gen` tool; resized to 1024 × 1024 for Xcode.

## Prompt

Use case: style-transfer. Asset type: production iPhone app icon, 1024 by 1024 square, fully opaque. Edit the provided open-book icon. Preserve its extremely simple recognizable geometry: just the outer outline of two open pages and a central spine, with empty interiors and no page writing. Rotate the BOOK symbol exactly 30 degrees clockwise in the image plane, keeping the square canvas upright, the book centered, and all edges comfortably inside the canvas. Turn the book outline and spine into smooth translucent sculpted glass with a subtle rounded bevel, clear polished highlights, delicate refraction, and restrained depth. Use rich autumn colors flowing through the glass: amber, burnt orange, copper, warm gold, and russet red. Keep the icon very minimalist and legible at small sizes. Background: a clean, warm ivory field filling the entire square; a very faint soft contact shadow is acceptable to reveal the glass. No extra objects, no leaves, no extra page lines, no letters, no text, no border, no rounded canvas corners, no busy scenery or texture. The result should still be one spare open-book sketch, now tilted and made of autumn-colored glass.
